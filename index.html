05 · Evolution

From an initial insight to a global platform.

ScoutHalo did not begin as a fully formed product. It evolved through research, iteration, customer conversations, and a deeper understanding of production workflows.

Origin

The location problem.

Location information was fragmented, inconsistent, and difficult to convert into production decisions.

First

NYCScout.

An initial experiment focused on location discovery in New York.

Learn

Production workflows.

Discovery alone was not enough. Teams needed evaluation, comparison, logistics, and preparation.

Now

ScoutHalo.

A broader operating system for production location intelligence.

Next

Build the category.

Better search, stronger data, agency adoption, and a global intelligence layer for production.

06 · Current priorities

The work that compounds.

The objective is not to add more features. It is to make ScoutHalo more relevant, more reliable, and more valuable to production teams.

01

Search relevance

Interpret creative intent accurately and rank locations based on visual fit, geographic context, production viability, and user constraints.

02

Data quality

Transform fragmented geographic, visual, environmental, and operational information into trustworthy production intelligence.

03

Production reasoning

Help teams identify tradeoffs, constraints, risks, and unknowns before committing time and money to a location.

04

Agency adoption

Work directly with production companies and creative agencies to validate the product inside real scouting and decision workflows.

05

Revenue

Convert product value into durable commercial relationships with the teams that experience the problem most acutely.
