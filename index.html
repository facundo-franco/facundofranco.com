// 01 — The Thesis

What is an
Agent Operator?

Most companies are about to face the same problem: they need to redesign their workflows for agents, not for people. But they don't have anyone who understands both the business function and the AI layer well enough to make that transition work.

The agent operator fills that gap. They walk into a function — marketing, ops, growth, retention — map how it works, and build the agent stack that runs it. No engineering degree required. Business judgment + AI execution.

Harry Stebbings, Jason Calacanis, Aaron Levie, and Greg Isenberg have all pointed to this role as one of the most in-demand positions of the next five years. 500K–1M jobs being created. The window to get in early is now.

Bottleneck: judgment
