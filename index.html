// 01 — ScoutHalo

Building the future
of location scouting.

Location scouting remains one of the most fragmented and manual workflows in production. Teams move between Google Maps, social platforms, location libraries, spreadsheets, PDFs, emails, local contacts, and personal knowledge just to identify and evaluate viable options.

ScoutHalo is building the intelligence layer for that workflow. It gives production teams a structured environment to discover locations, analyze their suitability, compare alternatives, organize shortlists, and prepare for execution.

The objective is not to replace location scouts. It is to give scouts, producers, filmmakers, photographers, and creative agencies better tools and faster access to the information required to make production decisions.

01
Discover
Search for locations using creative intent, visual characteristics, geographic requirements, and production constraints—not only names and categories.
02
Evaluate
Understand access, timing, lighting, permits, logistics, crowd conditions, limitations, and other factors that determine whether a location can actually work.
03
Decide
Compare options, organize shortlists, align stakeholders, and move from visual inspiration to a production-ready decision.
