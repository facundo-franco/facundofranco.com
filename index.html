01 / ScoutHalo

The location intelligence layer for creative production.

ScoutHalo helps production teams move from a creative brief to viable, shoot-ready locations with better search, deeper intelligence, and fewer operational surprises.

Location scouting is still fragmented.

Teams move between maps, social platforms, spreadsheets, PDFs, location libraries, emails, and local knowledge to answer basic production questions.

ScoutHalo centralizes that workflow. It helps teams discover locations, assess production constraints, compare alternatives, organize shortlists, and prepare decisions in one workspace.

The product is designed for location scouts, producers, photographers, filmmakers, production companies, and creative agencies.

03 / About

Founder, operator, and product builder.

I am Facundo Franco, the founder and CEO of ScoutHalo.

I am building ScoutHalo because the information needed to discover and evaluate production locations remains fragmented, inconsistent, and difficult to use.

My work spans product strategy, search quality, AI systems, data infrastructure, customer discovery, positioning, and founder-led go-to-market.

Before ScoutHalo, I worked across growth and operations, managing acquisition, CRM, analytics, marketplaces, commercial execution, and operational systems.

I am based in Uruguay and building for production teams and creative agencies operating across the United States, Europe, and global production markets.
