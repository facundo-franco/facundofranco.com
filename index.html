01 · ScoutHalo

The intelligence layer for production locations.

Production teams still scout across maps, social platforms, location libraries, spreadsheets, PDFs, and fragmented local knowledge. ScoutHalo brings that workflow into one structured, AI-powered workspace.

From a creative brief to a viable location.

ScoutHalo helps production teams search with intent, understand whether a location can actually work, compare alternatives, and prepare decisions with fewer unknowns.

It is designed for location scouts, producers, photographers, filmmakers, production companies, and creative agencies.
